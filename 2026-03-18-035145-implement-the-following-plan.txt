  Even the worst case is under 130K for input. But Step 4+5 merged means the
  output is also larger (component mapping reasoning + all user stories), and
  the LLM is doing two cognitive tasks: matching components AND generating
  creative story text. With GLM degrading around 130K, you'd want headroom.

  My recommendation: Keep Step 4 separate. Three reasons:

  1. Verification gate. Component mapping is the highest-leverage error. A wrong
   mapping propagates into every story for that process. A separate checkpoint
  lets you spot-check before burning tokens on story generation.
  2. Prompt focus. Step 4 is extraction/matching (low temperature,
  deterministic). Step 5 is creative generation (moderate temperature). Mixing
  modes hurts both, which you already observed in req/user-stories.md.
  3. Step 5 context gets leaner. Instead of 100k chars of components, Step 5
  receives only the ~10-15 relevant component definitions (~15-20k chars). This
  gives more headroom for the large opis files and for quality story output.
